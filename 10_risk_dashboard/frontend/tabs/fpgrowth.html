Itemset Support Distribution

Research question: How frequently do different drug combinations co-occur in patient records, and how does support vary across combination sizes? Support = the proportion of patients who received all drugs in a given itemset. The distribution reveals whether frequent patterns are driven by a few universally common medications or spread across many specific combinations. High-support itemsets are the strongest drug co-occurrence signals in this cohort.

Drug Association Network

Research question: Which drugs are most strongly associated with each other in this cohort, and do these associations cluster into polypharmacy groups? Nodes = drugs; edges = association rules (confidence: if a patient takes Drug A, they also take Drug B). Edge thickness/color reflects rule strength (lift). Dense clusters indicate polypharmacy combinations — groups of drugs that are routinely prescribed together — which may represent treatment regimens, drug interaction risks, or shared therapeutic targets relevant to the adverse outcome.

Research question: Which drug itemsets have the highest co-occurrence support within each density stratum, and do those patterns differ across low/medium/high/extreme? Toggle between grouped bars and a full matrix heatmap. Itemsets are ranked by peak support across all bins.
