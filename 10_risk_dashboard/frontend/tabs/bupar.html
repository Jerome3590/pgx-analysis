Drug-specific visuals

These panels are driven by the cohort and age band dropdowns above. Event-to-target BupaR visuals (all activity types) are kept separate.

Sequences to Target Outcomes (drugs)

Research question: Which drug sequences (ordered activity traces) most frequently precede the adverse outcome? Uses process mining to extract and rank the top ordered drug-activity sequences observed in patients who reached the target event, revealing common medication pathways that culminate in an ED visit or polypharmacy event.

Overall Activity Frequency (drugs)

Research question: Which drugs are most commonly prescribed in this cohort across the full training window (2016–2018)? Counts all pharmacy/dispensing events per drug name to establish the baseline utilization profile. Use the year filter to detect year-over-year shifts in prescribing patterns that may indicate clinical practice changes or formulary updates.

Pre-Target Activity Frequency (drugs)

Research question: Which drugs are most heavily prescribed in the period before the target outcome event? Isolates only events that occurred prior to the first adverse outcome, revealing the pre-event medication profile. Comparing this to Overall Activity Frequency highlights which drugs are disproportionately concentrated in the pre-event window.

Post-Target Activity Frequency (drugs)

Research question: How does the prescribing profile change after the adverse outcome? Counts drug events occurring after the target event to capture treatment responses, escalations, or new medication introductions following an ED visit or adverse event. Drugs that spike post-target may reflect reactive clinical interventions.

Trace Explorer (top 20 traces, drugs)

Research question: What are the most common ordered drug sequences across all patients in this cohort? Each trace is a unique ordered sequence of drug activities for a patient; the top 20 most frequent are shown ranked by prevalence. Use the year filter to see whether dominant medication sequences shift across 2016–2018, indicating evolving prescribing norms.

Trace Explorer Pre-Target (drugs)

Research question: Which ordered drug sequences most often immediately precede the adverse outcome? Restricts trace analysis to the pre-event window only, revealing the top 20 medication orderings that most frequently lead into a target event. Cross-reference with Target Pathway Patterns (DTW) to validate findings across both analytical frameworks.

Process Matrix (Drug × Drug)

Research question: Which drugs tend to follow other drugs in patient treatment journeys? Each cell shows the number of times Drug B was observed directly after Drug A across the cohort. High-frequency transitions (darker cells) indicate common medication handoffs — e.g., from an opioid to a benzodiazepine — that may signal escalation, co-prescribing patterns, or treatment substitutions relevant to the target outcome.

Activity Rate by Event Density Bin

Research question: Which drug codes have the highest activity rate within each density stratum, and do those profiles differ across low/medium/high/extreme? Toggle between grouped bars (bins on X, one bar group per activity) and a full matrix heatmap (activities × bins). Codes are ranked by peak rate across all bins.
