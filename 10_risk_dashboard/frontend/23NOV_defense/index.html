Appendix A1: Exact 5-Set Horn Clause Equations & PySAT Hitman Complexity

Appendix A1 · Computer Science & Symbolic Logic (Ch. 2–4) Step 8 _explode_tree_to_dataframe · PySAT Hitman(solver="m22")

CNF Path Compilation & Horn-Clause Representation

  • Vectorized Decision-Path Extraction (_explode_tree_to_dataframe):
    • Each root-to-leaf path \(p\) across 300 trees (CatBoost, XGBoost, Xgboost RF) is compiled into a conjunction of threshold literals \(L_k \equiv \mathbb{I}(x_{j_k} \lessgtr \tau_k)\) predicting class \(\hat{y}_p \in \{0, 1\}\): \[\bigwedge_{k=1}^{d_p} L_k(\mathbf{x}) \implies (\hat{y}_p = 1) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \left(\bigvee_{k=1}^{d_p} \neg L_k(\mathbf{x})\right) \vee (\hat{y}_p = 1)\]
  • Minimum-Cardinality Hitting Set (PySAT Hitman):
    • Given the collection \(\mathcal{P}_i\) of active positive-class paths firing on patient \(i\), the Abductive Explanation (\(\text{AXp}\)) is the minimal hitting set \(S^* \subseteq \bigcup_{p \in \mathcal{P}_i} \text{Literals}(p)\) solved via MiniSAT (m22): \[S^* = \arg\min_{S} |S| \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \forall p \in \mathcal{P}_i,\; S \cap \text{Literals}(p) \neq \emptyset\]
