How to use the PGx Risk Assessment Dashboard. All content stays on this page.

Overview

This dashboard supports F1120 Opioid ED Visit risk (ages 13–64) and Polypharmacy risk (ages 65–114). Model selection is automatic based on the age you enter. Ages 95–114 are treated as age band 85–94 due to cohort size.

Tabs

Main tabs: Risk Assessment | Drugs | ICD Codes | CPT Codes | Causal Analysis | DTW Trajectories | FP-Growth Patterns | BupaR Process Mining | PGx Patient Card | Documentation

  • Risk Assessment — Enter age, select codes (via Drugs / ICD / CPT tabs), then click Calculate Risk Score. Use Edit codes to change selections.
  • Drugs — Search and add drugs for your patient. Selections are used for the risk calculation. A visual display shows all selected drugs with option to remove them.
  • ICD Codes — Search and add ICD codes for your patient. For Opioid ED patients only (ages 13-64). Selections are used for the risk calculation.
  • CPT Codes — Search and add CPT codes for your patient. For Opioid ED patients only (ages 13-64). Selections are used for the risk calculation.
  • Causal Analysis — View causal importance and interaction plots by cohort and age band.
  • DTW Trajectories — Dynamic time warping trajectory visualizations.
  • FP-Growth Patterns — Frequent itemset and network visualizations.
  • BupaR Process Mining — Process mining and activity frequency charts.
  • PGx Patient Card — Generate a PGx card from SNP/phenotype input.

Workflow

  1. Set Age on Risk Assessment (this loads metadata and determines cohort/age band).
  2. Open Drugs tab, search and add the drugs you need. View selected drugs in the display area.
  3. For Opioid ED patients (ages 13-64), also open ICD Codes and CPT Codes tabs to add additional codes.
  4. Return to Risk Assessment and click Calculate Risk Score.
  5. Use Compare Scenarios to compare different code sets.
  6. Explore visualizations on other tabs (Causal Analysis, DTW, etc.) to understand feature importance and patterns.

Technical notes

The backend uses an ensemble of CatBoost, XGBoost, and XGBoost RF models. Metadata (drugs, ICD, CPT) is filtered by cohort and age band. All tabs use the same page; nothing here navigates you away to a separate doc page.

Note for Polypharmacy patients (ages 65-114): Only drugs are used for risk calculation. ICD and CPT code tabs are hidden for these patients.
