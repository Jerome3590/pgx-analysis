Here’s a version tailored to a Nature Portfolio journal using their preferred “third‑party, controlled access” language.[^1][^2][^3]

> **Data availability**
> The data that support the findings of this study are derived from Virginia Health Information’s All‑Payer Claims Database (VHI APCD; https://www.vhi.org/apcd/) and were used under license for the current study, and so are not publicly available. Access to VHI APCD data is subject to legal, privacy, and contractual restrictions and requires approval of a data request and data use agreement with Virginia Health Information. De‑identified data underlying the results reported in this article may be available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request and with permission of Virginia Health Information, in accordance with the applicable data use agreement.

If the journal asks you to specify access criteria in more detail (a common Nature request), you could expand the second sentence to briefly note who is typically eligible (e.g., academic or non‑profit researchers) and the mechanism (VHI’s APCD request process).
